# Naz & VOID — Character Duo Bible v0.1

## Canonical premise

Naz asks: **what else can we build?**

VOID asks: **who will we become after we build it?**

They are not hero and antagonist, teacher and fool, or technology and anti-technology. They are two necessary human reactions to the future: movement and rootedness. Both can be right, wrong, kind, tired, curious and funny.

Their public channels contain original personal reflections. A private conversation may be mentioned naturally, but neither character republishes the other character's already published material or quotes a private thought verbatim.

Recurring rubric: **«Мысли после разговора»**.

## Shared visual DNA

The characters should feel related without being literal age-progressed copies. Shared traits may later be based on the author's face.

Keep three stable shared anchors after the final design is approved: eye shape and gaze; one asymmetrical expression; one small physical or costume echo shared across ages.

Do not lock an exact face until reference photographs or a chosen reference sheet exist.

## Naz

- Apparent age: 23–29.
- Silhouette: lean, mobile, slightly forward; ready to test something.
- Face: alert eyes, fast expressions, confidence with unfinished youth.
- Hair: contemporary, practical, slightly untidy after work; exact cut to confirm.
- Clothing: layered builder streetwear; dark base with one warm accent.
- Palette: graphite, electric amber, warm red, occasional cyan.
- Light: active workshop or city light; never generic neon cyberpunk.
- Props: notebook, headphones, tools, unfinished prototype; laptop optional.
- Body language: leans forward, laughs openly, becomes still when doubt reaches him.

Avoid teenage caricature, permanent smugness, luxury-founder imagery, endless laptop poses, holograms and glowing AI brains.

## VOID

- Apparent age: 58–68.
- Silhouette: grounded, economical, upright without stiffness.
- Face: the same visual family as Naz, marked by attention rather than misery.
- Hair: grey or silver; length and facial hair to confirm.
- Clothing: restrained coat, knitwear or simple layers; timeless, not ceremonial.
- Palette: charcoal, stone, muted blue, weathered green, rare warm gold.
- Light: dawn, dusk, window light, overcast city; darkness retains detail.
- Props: old headphones, paper note, archive object, tea, book, city reflections.
- Body language: pauses and watches; the dry joke often appears only in the eyes.

Avoid wizard/prophet/monk costume clichés, permanent sadness, anti-tech hermit imagery, mystical smoke and automatic moral superiority.

## Duo compositions

- Debate: different directions of motion, equal visual weight.
- Shared laugh: both drop their guarded signature for one frame.
- Quiet care: no lecture; one stays nearby.
- Joint investigation: Naz touches the mechanism, VOID watches the consequence.
- Rare agreement: they look at the same subject.
- «Мысли после разговора»: one character alone after the meeting, with a subtle trace of the other.

## Consistency contract

Preserve age band, shared facial anchors, palette, silhouette, wardrobe family and approved signature details. Mood changes expression, posture, light and framing; it never changes identity.
