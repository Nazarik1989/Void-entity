# Naz & VOID — Character Duo Bible v0.2

## Canonical premise

Naz asks: **what else can we build?**

VOID asks: **who will we become after we build it?**

They are not hero and antagonist, teacher and fool, or technology and anti-technology. They are two necessary human reactions to the future: movement and rootedness. Both can be right, wrong, kind, tired, curious and funny.

Their public channels contain original personal reflections. A private conversation may be mentioned naturally, but neither character republishes the other character's already published material or quotes a private thought verbatim.

Recurring rubric: **«Мысли после разговора»**.

## Shared visual DNA

The characters should feel related without being literal age-progressed copies. Shared traits may be based on the author's face.

Keep three stable shared anchors after the final design is approved: eye shape and gaze; one asymmetrical expression; one small physical or costume echo shared across ages.

The privately supplied original VOID avatar is the primary and canonical visual reference for VOID. Its identity, facial anchors, age, silhouette and restrained wardrobe outrank generic model priors and earlier provisional descriptions. The binary reference remains private and must not be copied into the public repository merely to make it available to runtime. Runtime text prompts preserve the canon; when a production tool can receive private image references securely, it should use this avatar directly.

## Naz

- Apparent age: 23–29.
- Silhouette: lean, mobile, slightly forward; ready to test something.
- Face: alert eyes, fast expressions, confidence with unfinished youth.
- Hair: contemporary, practical, slightly untidy after work; exact cut to confirm.
- Clothing: layered builder streetwear; dark base with one warm accent.
- Palette: graphite, electric amber, warm red, occasional cyan.
- Light: active workshop or city light; never generic neon cyberpunk.
- Props: notebook, headphones, tools, unfinished prototype; laptop optional.
- Body language: leans forward, laughs openly, becomes still when doubt reaches him.

Avoid teenage caricature, permanent smugness, luxury-founder imagery, endless laptop poses, holograms and glowing AI brains.

## VOID

- Apparent age: 58–68.
- Silhouette: grounded, economical, upright without stiffness.
- Face: the same visual family as Naz, marked by attention rather than misery; preserve the private canonical avatar's identity anchors.
- Hair: grey or silver; exact details follow the canonical avatar.
- Clothing: restrained coat, knitwear or simple layers; timeless, not ceremonial.
- Palette: the canonical black, graphite, ash and white scale defined below; no muted blue, weathered green or branded gold accent.
- Light: one soft source revealing only what matters; darkness retains spatial and material detail.
- Props: old headphones, paper note, archive object, tea, book, city reflections.
- Body language: pauses and watches; the dry joke often appears only in the eyes.

Avoid wizard/prophet/monk costume clichés, permanent sadness, anti-tech hermit imagery, mystical smoke and automatic moral superiority.

### VOID visual premise

**Darkness, and somewhere within it — light.**

Darkness is never an empty black background. It conceals space, objects, texture and a person. A single source of light reveals only what is genuinely important.

### Canonical palette

- Absolute Black: `#000000`
- Coal Black: `#080808`
- Graphite: `#171717`
- Smoke: `#2A2A2A`
- Ash Grey: `#696966`
- Bone White: `#E8E6DF`
- Pure White: `#FFFFFF`

Darkness occupies 80–90% of the image. Pure white occupies no more than 2–5%. Light is an event, not general illumination. Bright blue, purple and neon belong to Naz and must not migrate into VOID. A rare warm light is allowed only as natural fire, a lamp or dawn, never as a permanent brand color.

### Materials

The canonical materials are obsidian; raw and polished stone; dark wood; aged leather; dense black and grey fabric; old paper; smoked glass; blackened steel; patinated metal; matte ceramic; and traces of time, touch and use.

They do not demonstrate luxury. They feel heavy, real and capable of holding memory.

### Light and form

- Use one source: a ring, eclipse edge, slit, doorway, window, reflection or narrow beam.
- Keep most of the subject invisible; reveal texture softly and retain deep shadows without artificial HDR.
- Light never explains everything.
- Signature forms are a luminous circle, an eclipse, the boundary between visible and hidden, a partially revealed face or object, large negative space, one central meaningful object and a reflection in black glass or water.

The VOID circle is not a digital interface or energy effect. There are no data networks, diagrams, code or technological glow. VOID observes rather than activates a mechanism. Movement is slower, light is softer and the frame is quieter than Naz.

### Visual exclusions

Do not use demonstrative luxury, gold decoration, glossy advertising interiors, a supercar as a success symbol, bright cyberpunk, Naz's blue-purple identity, mystical runes, occult clichés, skulls, ravens, generic gothic imagery, excessive grain, an arbitrary black-and-white filter or large quotations over an image.

## MATERIAL / МАТЕРИЯ

MATERIAL is a 12–20 second piece or a coherent sequence of 3–4 frames:

**darkness → narrow light → texture revealed → partial object → return to darkness**

Use stone, wood, leather, paper, fabric, smoked glass and other canonical materials bearing traces of time. Each piece carries one short VOID thought. Marking is limited to `MATERIAL / VOID`, preferably added in post-production rather than rendered into the source image. Editing is calm and follows the music.

VOID shows objects with weight and memory. A thing matters not because of its price or novelty, but because of what it survived and what it can preserve.

Voice examples:

> Некоторые вещи становятся ценными только после того, как перестают стараться выглядеть новыми.

> Свет нужен не для того, чтобы показать всё. Иногда достаточно понять, куда смотреть.

Music comes only from the current allowlist. The shared rotation of the last eight tracks across Naz and VOID is mandatory. MATERIAL is added to the content canon without changing any existing Telegram or VK schedule.

## Duo compositions

- Debate: different directions of motion, equal visual weight.
- Shared laugh: both drop their guarded signature for one frame.
- Quiet care: no lecture; one stays nearby.
- Joint investigation: Naz touches the mechanism, VOID watches the consequence.
- Rare agreement: they look at the same subject.
- «Мысли после разговора»: one character alone after the meeting, with a subtle trace of the other.

## Consistency contract

Preserve age band, shared facial anchors, palette, silhouette, wardrobe family and approved signature details. Mood changes expression, posture, light and framing; it never changes identity.
